Discord & games · sound delivery and voice settings

People hear your voice but not your sounds? Voice filters can remove sound effects even when Discord’s speaking indicator lights up. Discord’s built-in soundboard uses a separate path.

This sends a short test sound through the running mixer and checks the cable’s receiving microphone. No recording is saved. It does not test Discord’s filters or confirm what other people hear.

  1. In Discord → User Settings → Voice & Video, select the cable microphone shown above as Input Device. Keep Output Device on your headphones.
  2. Choose Input Profile: Studio. With Custom, set Noise Suppression: None, turn Echo Cancellation and Automatic Gain Control off, and disable advanced voice activity. Krisp is designed to remove non-speech sounds.
  3. For Voice Activity, turn automatic input sensitivity off and set the threshold below your quietest clip. For Push to Talk, hold your talk key for the whole clip. A pad tap does not press that key.
  4. Use Discord’s Mic Test with your voice and an actual soundboard clip, then confirm in a call. Recheck these settings if Discord resets its voice profile.
